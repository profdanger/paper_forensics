Evaluation methodology

Six dimensions, no single hype score

Evidence before inference

Figure-specific measurements, experimental uncertainty, controls and alternative hypotheses are separated from authors’ conclusions.

History before hindsight

Need, approach, benefits and competition are judged at publication time and separately in the present day.

Impact without citation shortcuts

Independent replications and contradictions are weighted more than citations, derivative applications or retrospective popularity.

Scores evaluate conceptual originality, methodological rigor, strength of evidence, performance advance, independent validation, and long-term impact. “Not rated” is preferable to false precision.
